Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ legal_updated_date }}

This Privacy Policy explains what information SecureVault collects, why, and how it is used, stored, and shared, in connection with our document-vault and secure-sharing Service. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account information

  • Name and email address, provided at registration or via Google Sign-In;
  • A securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text) and, separately, a hashed vault password used to protect your documents;
  • Profile picture, if you sign in with Google;
  • Email verification codes and password-reset codes, which are hashed and expire automatically.

b) Documents and vault content

  • The files you upload — which may include sensitive identity documents (e.g. Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence), certificates, or medical records — together with filenames, file type, size, and folder organization;
  • Where the Vault AI / search feature is used, extracted text and derived vector embeddings of your documents, used to power search and question-answering within your own vault.

Documents you upload are personal — and in many cases sensitive — data. We treat them accordingly: they are only shown to you, and to anyone you deliberately grant access to through a share link or QR code you generate.

c) Sharing and access data

  • Settings you choose for share links and QR codes: expiry date, view limit, download and print permissions, and an optional link password;
  • Access logs for shared links — action type (view, download, scan, failed password attempt), timestamp, IP address, and user-agent string of the visitor — so you can review who accessed a shared document and when.

d) Account and security activity

  • An audit log of account-security events (e.g. login, password change, registration, account deletion) including timestamp, IP address, and user-agent, used for security monitoring and to power the Activity page you can view in your account.

e) Technical information

  • Standard web request metadata such as IP address, browser/user-agent, and timestamps, collected automatically as part of operating the Service and enforcing rate limits that protect accounts from abuse;
  • Session cookies required to keep you logged in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and to verify your email address;
  • To store, display, organize, and let you download your documents;
  • To generate and operate share links and QR codes according to the settings you choose, and to log access to them so you can monitor and revoke access;
  • To power the optional Vault AI search/question-answering feature over your own documents;
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse (for example, rate-limiting login and sharing endpoints, and recording failed password attempts on share links);
  • To send you account-related emails: email verification codes, password-reset codes, and similar service notices. We do not send marketing email.

3. Third Parties We Use

We do not sell your personal data. We rely on the following categories of third-party service providers to operate SecureVault, each of which processes data only as needed to provide their service to us:

  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to log in or register with Google, Google verifies your identity and shares your email, name, and profile picture with us as permitted by your Google account settings.
  • Groq (AI provider) — if you use Vault AI, your question and relevant excerpts of your document text are sent to Groq's API to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use the AI feature.
  • Email delivery (Gmail SMTP) — used to deliver verification codes and password-reset codes to your email address.
  • Database and hosting infrastructure — your account and document data is stored in a PostgreSQL database operated on infrastructure we control or on a hosting provider we have contracted with to run the Service.

We do not share your documents or account data with any other third party for their own marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Sharing Documents With Others

Anyone who has a share link or scans a QR code you generate — and, where set, enters the correct link password — can view or download the shared document, subject to the permissions and expiry you configured. This sharing is initiated by you; we do not share your documents with anyone unless you create a link or QR code for that purpose. You can stop sharing, delete, or regenerate a link at any time from your Shared Documents page or Activity log, which immediately invalidates the previous link.

5. Data Retention

  • Account, document, and sharing data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Email verification codes and password-reset codes expire automatically (typically within minutes) and are marked used after a successful or failed verification, as applicable.
  • Share-access logs and account audit logs are retained to support the Activity page and for security purposes, and are deleted when the related account is deleted.
  • If you delete your account, your vaults, documents, files, QR code images, share links, and associated logs are permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone.

6. Security

We use measures including password hashing (bcrypt/Werkzeug), hashed and time-limited verification/reset codes, CSRF protection on state-changing requests, and rate limiting on sensitive endpoints (login, registration, password reset, sharing actions). Where configured by the operator, extracted document text used for AI search can additionally be encrypted at rest. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, and you should choose strong, unique passwords for your account and vault.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can access, update, or delete your documents and vaults at any time from My Vault.
  • You can view and revoke share links at any time from Shared Documents or the Activity page.
  • You can review your account and share-access history from the Activity page.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings.
  • Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law (such as the right to access, correct, or export your personal data, or to object to certain processing). Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the Service.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your data rights, contact us using the address published on this site's support or about page.
